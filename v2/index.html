THE PORTABLE PIANO

PIANO DE VOYAGE

The Piano de Voyage is a modular, portable piano keyboard focused on portability.
Made for the traveling musician, it's a piano keyboard that will fit in your backpack or carry-on bag.

MADE FOR THE MUSICIANS

Its unique assembly mechanism ensures a sturdy and durable connection between the keyboard modules. The Piano de Voyage can therefore be played on a non flat surface such as a X-Stand.

MODULAR

As opposed to regular or foldable keyboards, the Piano de Voyage is made of modules so you can adjust the size of your keyboard to you needs
Or the room left in your luggage

FEATURES


• USB-B connector for power and MIDI connectivity (low power consumption : works with iPhone or iPad)
• Jack socket for connecting a sustain pedal (6.35 mm - 1⁄4 in)
• 2x MIDI assignable knobs (volume or effects control)
• 4x MIDI assignable buttons (octave shift, program change...)
• 1x MIDI assignable 4-axis joystick (pitch-bend)
• Includes a 16Mb General MIDI soundbank with 100+ sampled instruments to play with your Piano de Voyage without the need for connecting a MIDI device
